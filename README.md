# Outerview

A research lab building world models.

Outerview is a research lab focused on understanding the physical world at planetary scale.

We are building systems that can organize the world’s physical information and make it accessible and usable — transforming raw imagery, video, location, and spatial context into knowledge that people and machines can search, interpret, and act on.

Our belief is simple: the physical world should be as searchable and understandable as the digital world.

# Outerview Permits

Open and normalized permit data from public and licensed sources, organized for
research, discovery, and analysis of the built environment.

This repository is the parent directory for Outerview permit datasets. It
explains the available permit categories and links to country-specific
repositories as coverage is published.

## Dataset overview

- Publisher: Outerview
- Category: Permits
- License: MIT
- Formats: CSV, JSON, GeoJSON, and Parquet where available
- Coverage: Country and regional coverage is documented in the linked country repositories
- Freshness: Varies by source; each country repository documents its update information

## Permit categories

The directory currently includes 54 permit categories. These permit types help
researchers, planners, developers, journalists, and software teams understand
construction activity, property changes, infrastructure work, and land-use
decisions.

- **Accessory dwelling unit permit** — authorizes a secondary residential unit on a property.
- **Addition permit** — covers an expansion or extension of an existing building.
- **Alteration permit** — documents significant changes to an existing structure.
- **Antenna permit** — covers the installation or modification of antennas and related equipment.
- **Battery storage permit** — covers stationary battery and energy-storage installations.
- **Boiler permit** — covers the installation or replacement of building boilers.
- **Carport permit** — authorizes a covered vehicle structure.
- **Change of use permit** — records a change in the permitted use of a property or space.
- **Commercial permit** — covers permit activity for commercial buildings and businesses.
- **Construction permit** — authorizes general building or construction work.
- **Curb cut permit** — covers a new or modified driveway connection to a street.
- **Deck permit** — covers construction or alteration of an exterior deck.
- **Demolition permit** — authorizes the removal or destruction of a structure.
- **Door permit** — covers installation or replacement of exterior doors.
- **Driveway permit** — covers construction or modification of a driveway.
- **Electric vehicle charger permit** — covers installation of electric vehicle charging equipment.
- **Electrical permit** — covers electrical installation, alteration, or repair work.
- **Elevator permit** — covers elevator or lift installation and related work.
- **Excavation permit** — covers excavation, earth removal, or below-grade work.
- **Fence permit** — covers construction or alteration of a fence.
- **Fire alarm permit** — covers installation or modification of fire alarm systems.
- **Fire permit** — covers fire-safety-related construction or operational work.
- **Fire suppression permit** — covers sprinklers and other fire suppression systems.
- **Foundation permit** — covers foundation construction or structural support work.
- **Garage permit** — covers construction or alteration of a garage.
- **Gas permit** — covers gas appliance, piping, or service work.
- **Generator permit** — covers installation of standby or emergency generators.
- **Grading permit** — covers grading, leveling, and site preparation.
- **Irrigation permit** — covers landscape irrigation system work.
- **Mechanical permit** — covers heating, ventilation, and mechanical systems.
- **Mobile home permit** — covers placement or alteration of a mobile or manufactured home.
- **New building permit** — authorizes construction of a new building.
- **Occupancy permit** — records authorization to occupy or use a building or space.
- **Patio permit** — covers construction or alteration of a patio.
- **Plumbing permit** — covers plumbing installation, alteration, or repair work.
- **Pool permit** — covers construction or alteration of a swimming pool.
- **Porch permit** — covers construction or alteration of a porch.
- **Residential permit** — covers permit activity for residential properties.
- **Retaining wall permit** — covers construction of a retaining wall.
- **Right-of-way permit** — covers work affecting a public right of way.
- **Roofing permit** — covers roof replacement, repair, or installation.
- **Scaffold permit** — covers temporary scaffolding and related structures.
- **Sewer connection permit** — covers connection to a public or private sewer system.
- **Shoring permit** — covers temporary or permanent support for excavation or structures.
- **Sidewalk permit** — covers sidewalk construction, repair, or modification.
- **Sign permit** — covers installation or alteration of signs.
- **Solar permit** — covers solar panel and related energy-system installations.
- **Structural permit** — covers work affecting a building’s structural elements.
- **Temporary event permit** — covers temporary events requiring site or occupancy approval.
- **Temporary structure permit** — covers tents, temporary buildings, and similar structures.
- **Tenant improvement permit** — covers improvements to an occupied commercial or residential space.
- **Water connection permit** — covers connection to a public or private water system.
- **Window permit** — covers installation or replacement of windows.
- **Zoning permit** — records zoning review or authorization for a proposed use or development.

See the complete [permit type index](PERMIT_TYPES.md) for the publication list.

## Permit data for research and discovery

Search and analyze permit records to understand local construction permits,
building permits, renovation activity, development patterns, property changes,
and infrastructure projects across cities and countries.

- What permits were issued in a given area?
- Where is new construction occurring?
- Which building permit categories are most common?
- How has permitting activity changed over time?
- Which projects have recent status changes?
- Where are residential and commercial developments concentrated?
- Which neighborhoods show new solar, electrical, plumbing, or roofing work?
